New "Tether" visual language, drawn from the kit's name: a sky in two themes, a slate night (dark) and a pale grey day (light), with agents on lines back to you. The palette is neutral slate with almost no colour: "needs you" is shown by contrast (a solid white pill in dusk, ink in dawn, a bright taut line, a solid primary button), and red is the only colour, for failures, high risk, Deny and removed diff lines. Titles use Instrument Serif, the interface Inter Tight, commands JetBrains Mono (system fallbacks in the tokens). Panels are translucent "night glass" (`.cr-glass`, `.cr-surface`) and turn solid under `prefers-reduced-transparency`, `prefers-contrast: more` or `data-transparency="reduce"`.

New `FleetSky` organism and `skyAgent(run)` selector: your agents as birds on lines back to you; an agent that needs you flies back on a taut, glowing line. Name tags are real buttons, arrivals are announced, and nothing moves under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
