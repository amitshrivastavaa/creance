New "Tether" visual language, drawn from the kit's name: a sky in two themes, dusk (dark) and dawn (light), with agents on lines back to you. Aurora teal is the one bright colour and is reserved for "needs you" (the taut line, the request edge, the primary action); moonlight is running, sage is done, coral is failed, lilac is blocked and medium risk. Titles use Instrument Serif, the interface Inter Tight, commands JetBrains Mono (system fallbacks in the tokens). Panels are translucent "night glass" (`.cr-glass`, `.cr-surface`) and turn solid under `prefers-reduced-transparency`, `prefers-contrast: more` or `data-transparency="reduce"`.

New `FleetSky` organism and `skyAgent(run)` selector: your agents as birds on lines back to you; an agent that needs you flies back on a taut, glowing line. Name tags are real buttons, arrivals are announced, and nothing moves under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
